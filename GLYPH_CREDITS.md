# Doodle stroke credits

Tegeera currently ships three **provisional, not human-reviewed** sketches from
Google's [Quick, Draw! Dataset](https://github.com/googlecreativelab/quickdraw-dataset),
made available under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
The original strokes were made by anonymous Quick, Draw! players. Tegeera
reduced and normalized their strokes to its 100×100 glyph format and ink style.
The 64-pixel previews passed a blind model-recognition screen; that is **not**
human visual approval or proof of educational accuracy.

- Bee — [source drawing](https://storage.googleapis.com/quickdraw_dataset/full/simplified/bee.ndjson#key_id=6401386805723136)
- Cup — [source drawing](https://storage.googleapis.com/quickdraw_dataset/full/simplified/cup.ndjson#key_id=5044554690461696)
- Flower — [source drawing](https://storage.googleapis.com/quickdraw_dataset/full/simplified/flower.ndjson#key_id=5157573114200064)

The human-approved offline pack remains empty. Provisional sketches are stored
in a separate manifest and are visibly identified in the drawing UI.
